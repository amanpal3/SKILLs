## Template 4 — 5-Touch Outreach Sequence

```markdown
# Outreach Sequence: [Campaign Name]
ICP: [Persona] | Goal: [Book meeting / trial signup] | Length: 5 touches

## Touch 1 — Cold Email (Day 0)
Subject: [Specific to their company or role]
Format: AIDA — Attention → Interest → Desire → Action
CTA: [Book 20-min call / reply yes or no]

## Touch 2 — LinkedIn Connect (Day 2)
Message (300 char max): [Personalized observation + reason to connect]

## Touch 3 — Follow-Up Email (Day 5)
Subject: Re: [original]
Body: [Add new value — case study, insight, stat]

## Touch 4 — LinkedIn DM (Day 8)
Message: [Reference their content or company news]

## Touch 5 — Breakup Email (Day 12)
Subject: Re: [original]
Body: Low-pressure close. Leave door open. No hard sell.
```
